
Word: programming

Part of speech: verb
Definition 1: To enter a program or other instructions into (a computer or other electronic device) to instruct it to do a particular task.
Example: He programmed the DVR to record his favorite show.
Definition 2: To develop (software) by writing program code.
Example: I programmed a small game as a demonstration.
Definition 3: To put together the schedule of an event.
Example: Mary will program Tuesday’s festivities.
Definition 4: To cause to automatically behave in a particular way.
Example: The lab rat was programmed to press the lever when the bell rang.

Part of speech: noun
Definition 1: The designing, scheduling or planning of a radio or television program/programme.
Example: The network changed its programming to mess with DVRs again.
Definition 2: Brain-washing
Example: No example available.
Definition 3: The act of writing a computer program.
Example: Management wanted to know how much programming the project would need.
Definition 4: The software that controls a machine, or the logic expressed in such software; operating instructions.
Example: A robot's programming doesn't allow for love.
